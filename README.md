# PharmaLens 🔬 — Clinical Intelligence & AI Prescription Scanner

PharmaLens is an AI-powered pharmaceutical intelligence application and clinical research platform designed to transform medical prescriptions into real-time actionable insights.

Featuring on-device **Google ML Kit OCR**, authoritative **openFDA** adverse drug reaction monitoring, and real-time **ClinicalTrials.gov** search.

## 🌟 Key Features
- **On-Device ML Kit OCR**: Real-time optical scanning of medical prescriptions, drug slips, and discharge summaries with zero cloud latency.
- **openFDA Safety Engine**: Instant lookup of FDA boxed warnings, contraindications, and FAERS adverse drug reaction reports.
- **ClinicalTrials.gov Global Explorer**: Direct access to over 480,000 interventional studies, recruiting trials, and study sponsor pipelines.
- **Privacy First**: 100% on-device vision processing protects patient data and healthcare privacy.

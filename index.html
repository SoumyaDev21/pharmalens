Capabilities

Built with Regulatory Science & AI Precision

Designed from first principles to solve critical clinical challenges in medicine intake, adverse event tracking, and regulatory research.

Google ML Kit Prescription OCR

State-of-the-art on-device optical character recognition scans doctor prescriptions, hospital discharge summaries, and pharmaceutical labels with zero cloud latency.

  • Detects drug name, dosage, frequency, and administration route
  • Handles cursive handwriting and structured doctor slips
  • 100% on-device processing guarantees patient data privacy

Real-Time openFDA Safety Engine

Direct synchronization with the U.S. Food and Drug Administration API provides immediate access to authoritative adverse drug reactions and clinical monographs.

  • FAERS adverse reaction frequency & severity signals
  • Black-Box boxed warnings, contraindications & precautions
  • Drug-drug interaction cross-checks against concurrent regimens

ClinicalTrials.gov Global Explorer

Explore over 480,000 global clinical research protocols. Filter actively recruiting studies, oncology clinical trials, and study sponsor pipelines.

  • Investigate phase 1–4 clinical trial endpoints & enrollment
  • Track active trials for targeted therapies & biosimilars
  • Direct study IDs (NCT numbers), principal investigator contacts

Pharmacovigilance-Grade Architecture

Grounded in GxP, GVP (Good Pharmacovigilance Practice), and global safety frameworks. Designed by an industry pharmacovigilance specialist.

  • MedDRA terminology compatible data mapping
  • ICSR (Individual Case Safety Report) causality alignment
  • Empowers both healthcare professionals and empowered patients
